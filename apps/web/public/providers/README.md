# Company logos

`openai.svg` and `anthropic.svg` are the monochrome company marks from
[Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/a94750e3f5f8fc33757b839d85030e742284e43a/packages/static-svg/icons),
pinned to commit `a94750e3f5f8fc33757b839d85030e742284e43a` and copied without modification.
The upstream MIT license is retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Company names and logos
remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Serve these files locally. The table inverts the black marks in dark mode and keeps
provider names as text; the images are decorative to avoid duplicate accessible labels.
